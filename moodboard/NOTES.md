### What batch 1 says so far
- **Fashion and editorial, not toy-like.** The Playground direction's primary colours and rounded blocks are wrong for this client.
- **Mostly monochrome** (white, bone, grey, black) with one strong colour at a time: cobalt, green, red.
- **Type is the hero.** Swiss grotesk for systems, a high-contrast serif for voice, experimental display type for moments.
- **Archive and research language as style:** item numbers, sizes, years, measurements, indexes.
- **Photography:** faceless fashion, sculptural still life, flash-lit macro.
- **Motion:** systematic and generative (rings, grids), never bouncy.
