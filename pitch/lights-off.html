We create brands rooted by serious research and driven by playful imagination.

Every project runs on two tracks. Research keeps the work honest. Play keeps it memorable. A date brand or a record label gets the same running order: five acts, each with a serious job and a playful one.

The method, in five acts

  1. Act I

    Observe

    Serious
    Field research, audits, interviews
    Play
    Collect everything, judge nothing

  2. Act II

    Question

    Serious
    Find the tension worth solving
    Play
    Ask the silly question out loud

  3. Act III

    Play

    Serious
    Structured exploration sprints
    Play
    Make fifty wrong things quickly

  4. Act IV

    Test

    Serious
    Put ideas in front of real people
    Play
    Keep what makes people lean in

  5. Act V

    Launch

    Serious
    Systems, guidelines, rollout
    Play
    Leave room for the brand to keep playing
